## Portrait — aki-portrait.png

Use case: logo-brand / stylized-concept. Create a polished original mascot concept illustration for AnkiQuest, a flashcard study and learning quest app. Subject: Aki, an adorable small red panda adventurer, standing full body in a welcoming three-quarter pose. Distinctive recognizable silhouette: oversized rounded triangular ears, broad fluffy cheeks with cream red-panda face markings, tiny dark brown nose, large friendly dark eyes with simple highlights, short chubby limbs, and a large curved bushy tail with 5 alternating warm orange and dark russet rings. Warm rusty orange fur, cream muzzle and ear interiors, dark chocolate paws. Aki wears one short indigo-purple scarf tied at the side and a small indigo cross-body flashcard satchel; one cream flashcard visibly peeks out. Holds one plain cream flashcard in one paw and raises the other paw in a friendly little wave. Expression warmly curious and encouraging, emotionally appealing to adults as well as children. Art direction: beautiful professional app mascot branding, clean simple 2D graphic illustration with very restrained soft shading, smooth confident shapes, minimal dark outlines, excellent readability at small sizes, a cohesive limited color palette. Background solid warm ivory with a very subtle grounding shadow and plenty of clear space around the entire character. Square composition, centered character, tail and scarf completely in frame. No typography, no logos, no watermark, no other animals, no owl features, no green character, no realistic fur texture, no glossy 3D rendering, no busy scenery. This is a concept preview, not an app screenshot.
